# FlipForever 2.5 Beta 18

This is a test version. Only apps with *Get beta versions* turned on (About & updates) receive it.

**All your auction gold in one click.** Open your mailbox and press *Collect auction gold* next to it. It takes the gold from every sale and leaves everything else in your mail alone.

**Never miss a gear upgrade.** When a scan finds a new affordable upgrade for your character, you hear a sound and see it in chat.

**Cooldowns at a glance.** The combat helper's small icons now count down the seconds until each ability is ready.

**Where does my class quest start?** The Advisor now names the quest giver and where to find them. Once you've talked to them, clicking the step points the waypoint arrow there.

**Press + to fight.** The combat helper now listens to the + key (and numpad +) right away. Set your own key in the FlipForever window if you prefer another.

**Combat helper waits for cooldowns.** Your big abilities fire the moment they're ready, and the rest of the rotation only moves on after a spell was really cast. Nothing is skipped because it wasn't ready yet. The icon shows what your next press casts, and the small icons show your cooldowns.

**Best deals on top.** The shopping list in game now puts deals first and shows the expected profit per item.

**Which character earns the most?** With more than one trader, the app's Dashboard now shows profit per character.

**Forgot to equip something?** The Gear tab now also shows better gear already in your bags, with an Equip button.

**Smaller deposits.** Fast sellers now go up for 12 hours, where the deposit is smallest, and slow sellers get 48 hours to find a buyer.

**The Advisor speaks your language.** Its next steps (talents, quests, gear, dungeons, gold to save and more) now appear in your chosen language.

**Upgrades and crafts in the app.** The app's Advisor page now lists each character's best auction house gear upgrades and most profitable crafts. In game, new shopping lists are built faster after each scan.

**Waypoint arrow.** An arrow on screen shows the way and the distance: to the pin you place on your map, to a point you type with `/ffway 45 60`, or to the next stop on your gathering loop. It turns green when you're heading the right way. Drag it wherever you like, or switch it off in the FlipForever window.

**More translated.** The FlipForever window's settings, the Advisor window and the profession planner now appear in your language too.

**9 builds for every class.** The Advisor now offers every build the current WoW: Forever guides publish: Method, Warcraft Tavern and RankedBoost. That covers leveling, dungeon and raid damage, tanking, healing and PvP for all specs. Each build shows its role, point split, talents, the guide's advice and its rotation, and the combat helper follows the build you pick. Pick one in the Advisor with the < > buttons.

**Also in 2.5:** combat helper from level 1 to 60, exact profession skill-up chances, vendor flips, sharper auction math, all 11 game languages, a gear finder, a crafting profit finder, and a redesigned app and in-game window.
