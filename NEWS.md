# FlipForever 2.4 Beta 4

This is a test version. Only apps with *Get beta versions* turned on (About & updates) receive it.

**Posting against dumped prices.** When someone lists one or two items far below everyone else, FlipForever now prices your auction against the rest of the market instead of following the dump down, and mentions it in chat. Your own auctions, on any of your characters, are still never undercut.

**Earlier in 2.4**
- The FlipForever window in game has tabs: Overview, Shopping list and Settings. The shopping list shows each item's latest price, your maximum, how many you've bought, and a Skip box.
- The desktop app has sidebar navigation, page titles, striped tables and colour-coded figures.
- The Gathering window matches the rest of FlipForever, and the Advisor names your most profitable farming zone so far.
