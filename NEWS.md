# FlipForever 2.4 Beta 1 - for testing

**This is a beta.** Only apps with *Get beta versions* ticked (About & updates) see it.

- 🗂️ **The FlipForever window has tabs now** (gold coin on the minimap): **Overview**, **Shopping list**, **Settings**.
- 🛒 **See your shopping list in game:** each item's price in your last scan (green = a deal right now), your max price, and how many you've bought.
- ⛔ **Skip box:** don't want an item? Tick *Skip*, and it's never bought on that character until you untick it.

Next in the GUI work: a cleaner, more modern look in the desktop app.
