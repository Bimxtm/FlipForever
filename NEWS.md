# FlipForever 2.4 Beta 4 - for testing

**This is a beta.** Only apps with *Get beta versions* ticked (About & updates) see it.

- 💸 **No more chasing dumped prices:** when someone lists 1-2 items far below everyone else, FlipForever prices your auction against the real market instead and tells you in chat. You still never undercut your own auctions, from any of your characters.
- ⛏️ **Earlier in 2.4:** the Advisor names your best farming zone, Gathering matches the FlipForever look, the app has a sidebar with colours that mean something, and in game the FlipForever window has tabs and a shopping-list view with a Skip box.
